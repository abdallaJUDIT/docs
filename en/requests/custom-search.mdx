The **Custom Search** allows you to perform queries based on specific filters, without the need to provide a CPF, CNPJ, OAB, or name as a starting point. We return lawsuits from all courts that meet the defined criteria.

This option is ideal for identifying lawsuits with certain characteristics across a wide range of jurisdictions.

<Warning>
    This type of query is available only through an **on-demand report**. If you are interested in this service, contact our [commercial](https://api.whatsapp.com/send/?phone=5521985284143) team.
</Warning>

<Steps>
    <Step title="Available filters" stepNumber="1">

    Below are the main filters that can be used in the custom search:

    - **Type of participation**: allows you to filter by lawsuits where the main party is the plaintiff, defendant, interested party, or undefined.

    - **Case value**: you can filter lawsuits with a case value above or below a certain amount.

    - **Courts**: allows you to include or exclude specific courts from the query, based on the list of supported courts.

    - **Procedural subjects**: allows you to include or exclude certain legal subjects, based on their official codes.

    - **Procedural classes**: allows you to filter lawsuits by specific class types (such as public civil actions, writs of mandamus, among others).

    - **Filing date**: allows you to define a minimum filing date for the lawsuits to be returned.

    - **Last case update date**: allows you to filter lawsuits based on the date of the last registered case update.

    - **Party names**: restricts the search to lawsuits that contain certain names of people or companies among the parties.

    - **Party documents**: restricts the search to lawsuits that contain certain CPFs or CNPJs in the parties of the lawsuit.

    - **Use of specific credentials**: allows you to associate the query with credentials previously registered in the [credentials vault](/en/essentials/cofre-de-credenciais), ensuring access to lawsuits that require authentication.


    </Step>
</Steps>
