title: "Create attachments ZIP export"
description: "Requests the export of every attachment of a lawsuit into a single ZIP file."
openapi: "openapi/requests.json POST /responses/{response_id}/attachments/exports"
api: "POST https://requests.production.judit.io/responses/{response_id}/attachments/exports"
icon: "file-zipper"
---

<Warning>
**Not available to all customers.** To enable exports on your account, contact support at [atendimento@judit.io](mailto:atendimento@judit.io).
</Warning>

The export is **asynchronous** and always starts with `status: "pending"`. The full flow has three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the export">
    Call this route with the lawsuit's `response_id`. Keep the returned `attachments_export_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the status">
    Call [Get attachments ZIP export](/en/api-reference/endpoint/attachments/attachment-export-status) until `status` changes to `completed` (or `failed`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the ZIP">
    Use the `attachments_zip_url` from the status call, or call [Download attachments ZIP](/en/api-reference/endpoint/attachments/attachment-export-download).
  </Step>
</Steps>
